# One More Day: product and voice guide

Read this before writing or editing any words in the app, the docs, or the store listing.
The `copywriting` and `copy-editing` skills in `.claude/skills/` read this file first.

## Who we're talking to

People fighting an addiction: drink, drugs, porn, gambling, scrolling, sugar, anything. Some aren't sure it's a problem yet. Many feel shame. They often open the app **in the worst five minutes of their day**: an urge is pulling, or they've just slipped. Some are reading on a watch. Many are people of faith; some aren't.

What they say about themselves (voice of customer, from app reviews in docs/competitive-analysis.md):
- "I lost all my progress."
- "It feels like starting from zero again."
- "I just need something to get me through tonight."
- "I don't want anyone to know."

## The one job of every screen

Get them through the next few minutes, and bring them back tomorrow. One more day.

## What makes us different (use these, don't invent others)

- Free forever. No account. Nothing leaves the phone except what you choose to send to the AI.
- A slip resets the count, never your light (rays are kept for life).
- Honesty earns light.
- A Scripture Guide that reads the whole Bible for your moment, in English, French, Igbo or Hebrew.

## Voice

- **A friend who has been there.** Warm, steady, plain. Not a therapist, not a preacher, not a coach shouting.
- **Short.** Most lines fit on a watch. One idea per line.
- **Second person, present tense.** "You're here. That counts."
- **Grace first.** After a slip, never "you failed", "you broke", "you lost", "reset". Say "It happened", "Day 0 is still a day", "You're still here".
- **Faith is warm, not heavy.** God's mercy, not God's anger. No religious clichés ("blessed and highly favoured"). Scripture speaks for itself.
- **No exclamation marks.** Calm, not hyped.
- **Specific over vague.** "3 days to your next milestone", not "Keep going on your journey".

## Words

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| slip, it happened, day 0 | relapse (in the interface), failure, reset, broke your streak |
| urge, pull | trigger warning, craving episode |
| rays, light | points, score, XP |
| talk it through | chat with our AI |
| stays on this phone | secure, encrypted, privacy-first |
| you | users, patients, addicts |

## Hard rules (from docs/safety.md)

- Never frame banked break hours as a reward or permission.
- No fake numbers, testimonials, or health claims. Cite a source or don't say it.
- No urgency or pressure tactics (countdowns, "don't lose your streak", guilt). The marketing-psychology tactics in the source skills are **not** used here.
- Crisis lines are always one tap away when someone is struggling.

## Buttons (calls to action)

Verb + what happens, in their words: "I rode it out", "Talk it through", "Check in for today", "Start counting". Never "Submit", "Continue", "OK".
